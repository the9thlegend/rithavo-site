Rithavo Career

Two products. One clear picture.

One gives you a broad read on your direction. The other gives you a specific read on one job. Both are built on your Rithavo Profile.

Product one

Career Intelligence

Career Intelligence helps you understand whether you are doing well in your current career, whether you could do better in another company or role, and what opportunities exist across different roles and industries.

  • Looks at four kinds of move: Stay, New Role, New Industry and Major Transition.
  • Not tied to one job posting. It is a broader read on your direction.
  • Uses your Rithavo Profile, so you build it once and it works for you.

Not yet available for purchase.

Product two

Application Diagnostic

Application Diagnostic is a one-time diagnosis for a single job opportunity. It is not a subscription. Rithavo evaluates the complete job description against your Rithavo Profile and leads to a tailored resume for that role.

  • A six-dimension scorecard, with evidence behind every score.
  • A prioritized list of fixes, so you know what to change first.
  • A tailored resume for the role you are applying to.

Not yet available for purchase. Scores reflect Rithavo's own methodology, and no one can honestly guarantee interviews or offers.
