What Rithavo is

Rithavo is a digital products and services company. These terms currently cover the Rithavo Career Application Diagnostic; terms for future products (Rithavo Systems, Rithavo Growth) will be added as those launch.

What the Application Diagnostic is

The Application Diagnostic is a structured, job-specific analysis of your resume against a role you're targeting. It is produced using a defined methodology across six scored dimensions, with multi-stage quality control before delivery. It is a diagnostic and improvement tool — it is not a human resume-writing service, a recruitment service, or a guarantee of any hiring outcome.

What we guarantee

We guarantee that your report is factually accurate and genuinely tailored to your specific resume and job description — not a generic template. If it isn't, we'll fix it or refund you.

We do not and cannot guarantee interviews, callbacks, shortlisting, or job offers. Hiring outcomes depend on many factors outside our — or any resume tool's — control, and any service claiming otherwise is not being honest with you.

Pricing and payment

The current price for the Application Diagnostic is ₹399, shown at the time you order. During this early stage, payment is made by direct UPI transfer using instructions sent privately by email after intake — not through an automated checkout on this website.

Turnaround

Reports are typically delivered within 24–48 hours of payment confirmation. This is a target, not a contractual deadline, and may occasionally shift with order volume — we'll tell you directly if a specific order will take longer.

Refunds

If your report is not factually accurate or not genuinely tailored to the resume and job description you submitted, tell us and we will fix it or refund your payment in full. Refunds are not offered on the basis of hiring outcomes, since we make no promises about those.

Your responsibilities

  • The resume and job description you submit should be your own and accurate.
  • The report is intended for your personal use in your own job search, not for resale or redistribution.
