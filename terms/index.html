Application Diagnostic

What it is

The Application Diagnostic is a structured, job-specific analysis of your resume against a role you are targeting. It is produced using a defined methodology across six scored dimensions, with multi-stage quality control before delivery. It is a diagnostic and improvement tool. It is not a human resume-writing service, a recruitment service, or a guarantee of any hiring outcome.
