Who it's for & how it's different

Two different questions. Two different products.

Application Diagnostic

"Is this specific resume aligned to this specific job?" A fast, job-by-job check — six dimensions, scored, with a fix list. Best when you already know what you're applying for.

Career Intelligence

"Where does my career actually stand, and where can it realistically go?" A structured assessment of your whole professional picture — evidence, capabilities, fit, gaps, and direction — not tied to one job posting.

Best for someone weighing a transition, a level change, or an industry move — not just polishing one application.

How Rithavo reaches its conclusions

Transparent, without pretending to be more than it is.

Your assessment combines the professional information you provide, the evidence in your profile, Rithavo's own curated role and industry requirements, and the Rithavo Career Intelligence framework.

Your ProfileExperience, education, capabilities, and evidence you've provided.
Career RequirementsRole expectations, industry requirements, and capability relationships Rithavo curates.
Rithavo AnalysisFit, gaps, transition potential, and readiness — computed by comparing the two above.
Your ResultRecommended directions, why they were recommended, confidence, and next actions.

Rithavo never searches the internet and never asks an AI model to "just judge" your resume. No external sources are cited today — a Knowledge Bank of curated external references is a planned next expansion, not part of this assessment yet.

What you provide

Current role, background, skills, industries, and career direction — in your own words. Nothing is guessed on your behalf, and nothing you didn't provide is ever claimed as evidence.
