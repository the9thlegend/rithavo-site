Why applications go quiet

Six reasons your resume might be losing before it's read.

01

Weak role alignment

Your resume reads for a different job than the one you're applying to.

02

Vague achievements

Responsibilities are listed, but not what you actually accomplished.

03

Keyword mismatch

Your language doesn't match the language in the job description.

04

Missing evidence

Claims aren't backed by scale, result, or context.

05

Unclear positioning

A reader can't quickly tell your level or your strongest edge.

06

Structural friction

Formatting that makes your resume harder to scan quickly.

We assess these directly against your resume and your target job. We don't make claims about how any specific company's ATS software works internally — no outside service can honestly know that.
