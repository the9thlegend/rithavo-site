Rithavo Business Transformation

Turning business potential
into measurable performance.

We work with businesses to strengthen operations, support delivery commitments and pursue growth opportunities. Every engagement begins with understanding your requirements.

Discuss Your Business Requirements

Your enquiry will be reviewed by Rithavo. If appropriate, a Rithavo specialist will get in touch to understand your requirements.

Where Rithavo can help

Four areas of work.

Sales and revenue operations

Improve the way your business identifies, qualifies and works with potential customers.

Rithavo can help with MQL and SQL lead requirements, lead research and the review of data you provide, working towards agreed lead-generation and research outcomes. Support for sales targets is possible where the scope and obligations are agreed separately.

Business process and operational management

Build greater structure and clarity into the way your business operates.

Rithavo can help with organisational structures, hierarchy management, business processes, Standard Operating Procedures and operational strategies.

Taking ownership of selected business functions

When delivery commitments become difficult to manage, additional operational support may be needed.

Depending on the engagement, Rithavo may provide additional resources, support an existing production team, or take operational control of an agreed production team, with agreed delivery responsibilities. Any transfer of operational responsibility is agreed in advance.

Business development and revenue growth

Work towards defined business-development and revenue-growth objectives.

This can include business-development support, revenue-growth initiatives and, where appropriate, performance-linked objectives. Targets, how they are measured, timeframes and commercial terms are agreed separately. Rithavo does not promise guaranteed growth or standard performance outcomes.

How engagements begin

Understanding comes first.

  1. Share your business requirement.
  2. Rithavo reviews your enquiry.
  3. If appropriate, a Rithavo specialist gets in touch to understand your requirements.
  4. The proposed scope and approach are discussed. Any scope of work is approved by Rithavo's Founder before it is agreed.
  5. Quotation, timelines and commercial terms are discussed.
  6. Work proceeds only after both parties agree.
Possible engagement models

The right model depends on your requirement.

  • Advisory or consultation
  • A defined project with agreed deliverables
  • Ongoing operational support
  • Dedicated resources or team deployment
  • End-to-end ownership of an agreed business function
  • A performance-linked engagement with agreed targets

These are possible engagement models, not standard packages. They are discussed once Rithavo understands your requirement.
