Privacy Policy.

What Rithavo collects, why, who helps us process it, and what you can ask us to do. Last updated 26 November 2026.

What this covers

This policy explains what information Rithavo collects when you use this website and our products, what we do with it, and what we do not do with it. It covers:

  • the Rithavo website and your use of it;
  • your Rithavo account and profile;
  • Career Intelligence and the Application Diagnostic;
  • Ritanya, our guided assistant;
  • enquiries about Product Studio and Business Transformation, and any message you send us.

Career Intelligence and the Application Diagnostic are not yet available for purchase. This policy already describes how your information is handled in them.

What we collect

Browsing the website

You can read the public pages without signing in. The site runs a small analytics layer of its own. It records which page was viewed and a small set of actions, a random identifier kept in a cookie called rv_sid that identifies a browser and not a person, and a coarse device type (mobile, tablet or desktop). It does not store your IP address, does not fingerprint your device, and is not shared with any third-party analytics or advertising service.

Your account

When you join Rithavo we collect your email address and the details needed to sign you in securely. If you sign in, the browser identifier above can be linked to your account in the same way an ordinary session cookie is.

Your profile

Your profile holds what you choose to add: for example your name, headline, experience, education, skills and a photograph. It is private to your account unless you choose to publish a public profile card. What you publish can be seen by anyone who has the link, and you can change or unpublish it.

Career Intelligence

To give you a read on your direction, Career Intelligence uses the career information you provide, such as your profile details or resume and what you tell us about your goals.

Application Diagnostic

When you request an Application Diagnostic you submit your information through our intake form: your name and email address, your resume, your target role, the job description you are targeting (or a description of it), and any optional context you choose to share, such as what is not working, your LinkedIn URL or your experience level. We do not collect this passively. Nothing is gathered until you submit the intake form yourself.

Ritanya

Ritanya answers questions using information approved by Rithavo. What you type is used to work out an answer. Please do not type sensitive personal information, such as passwords, payment details or government ID numbers, into the chat.

Enquiries and messages

Our contact, business enquiry, help and careers forms do not send anything to this website. They prepare an email in your own mail app or email service, and nothing reaches Rithavo until you press send there. When you do, we receive your email address and whatever you chose to write. Nothing on those forms is required.

Why we collect it

We use your information to run your account, to provide the product you asked for, to reply to you, and to keep the service secure and working. The information and documents you submit for an Application Diagnostic are used only to prepare your diagnostic and to communicate with you about your order. We do not sell your information, we do not reuse your resume or personal information for any other purpose, and we do not use it to train external systems.

Business enquiries are reviewed by Rithavo. If appropriate, a Rithavo specialist will get in touch to understand your requirements.

Third parties involved in processing

We use a small number of providers to run Rithavo. None of them is used for advertising or marketing.

  • Hosting and infrastructure. The website and our database and file storage run on third-party hosting and database services.
  • Email. Sign-in emails are sent, and hello@rithavo.com is hosted, through our email provider.
  • Tally. The Application Diagnostic intake form is built and hosted on Tally. Your submission, including your resume file, is processed and stored through Tally's platform before we retrieve it.
  • Google Sheets and Google Drive. We keep a private internal record of your submission (name, contact details, order details and status) in Google Sheets, and your generated diagnostic report and its underlying analysis in Google Drive. Both are accessible only to Rithavo and are not shared publicly or with any other party.
  • Anthropic. To prepare your Application Diagnostic, your resume and target job description are processed using Anthropic's Claude API, an AI language model service, to assist with analysis. The resulting analysis and report are reviewed by a person before anything is sent to you. Nothing is delivered without human review. Anthropic's commercial API terms state that data submitted through the API is not used to train their models by default.

Each provider's own handling of data is governed by its own privacy policy.

How we handle payment

During this early stage, payment is handled manually by UPI transfer, with instructions given to you directly after you submit the intake form. We do not collect card numbers, bank credentials or UPI PINs through our website or forms. Payment happens in your own banking app, outside anything Rithavo or our form provider operates. If we introduce an online payment service, card details will be handled by that service and not stored by Rithavo, and this policy will be updated before it goes live.

Consent

Before you can submit the Application Diagnostic intake form, you are asked to confirm that you consent to Rithavo processing the information and documents you have provided for the purpose of preparing your diagnostic. Submission is not possible without this confirmation.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as it is needed to run your account and to deliver and support your order. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing hello@rithavo.com. We will confirm once it has been removed from our records and, where applicable, from Tally.

Who has access

Your information is accessed only as needed to run the service, prepare and quality-check your diagnostic, and respond to you. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing.

Cookies and local storage

Rithavo uses only the cookies and local storage it needs:

  • a sign-in session cookie, so you stay signed in;
  • the first-party analytics cookie rv_sid described above;
  • small items stored in your browser so the site works as an installable app and remembers display choices.

The website does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking scripts. If that changes, this policy will be updated before any such tool goes live.

Your rights

  • You can ask what information we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to correct inaccurate information. You can also edit your profile yourself whenever you are signed in.
  • You can ask us to delete your information, subject to any order we are actively fulfilling for you.
  • You can withdraw your consent, and unpublish a public profile card, at any time.

To make any of these requests, email hello@rithavo.com.

Changes to this policy

As Rithavo adds products, this policy will be updated. Material changes will be reflected here with a new date at the top of the page. This version was last updated on 26 November 2026.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to hello@rithavo.com. You can also use the help and feedback page.
