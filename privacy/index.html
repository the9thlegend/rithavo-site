Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 August 2026

What this covers

This policy explains what information Rithavo collects when you use our website or services (currently the Rithavo Career Application Diagnostic), and what we do — and don't do — with it.

What we collect

When you request an Application Diagnostic, we collect what you submit directly: your name and email address, your resume, the job description you're targeting, and any optional context you choose to share (target role, target company, LinkedIn URL, or a note about your concerns). We do not collect this information through the website passively — nothing is gathered until you submit the intake form yourself.

Why we collect it

The information you submit and your resume are used only to prepare your Rithavo Application Diagnostic and to communicate with you about your order. We don't share, sell, or reuse your resume or personal information for any other purpose, and we don't use it to train external systems.

How we handle payment

During this early stage, payment is handled manually by UPI transfer, with instructions sent privately to you by email after intake. We do not collect card numbers, bank credentials, or UPI PINs through our website or forms — payment happens directly in your own banking app, outside of anything Rithavo operates.

How long we keep it

We retain your resume and intake information only as long as needed to deliver and support your order. You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing hello@rithavo.com or replying to your delivery email, and we'll confirm once it's removed.

Who has access

Your resume and information are accessed only as needed to prepare and quality-check your diagnostic. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use advertising cookies or third-party tracking scripts. If that changes as the site grows, this policy will be updated accordingly before any such tool goes live.

Your rights

  • You can ask what information we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • You can ask us to delete your information, subject to any order we're actively fulfilling for you.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to hello@rithavo.com.
