Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

What this covers

This policy explains what information Rithavo collects when you use our website or services (currently the Rithavo Career Application Diagnostic), and what we do — and don't do — with it.

What we collect

When you request an Application Diagnostic, you submit your information through our intake form: your name and email address, your resume, your target role, the job description you're targeting (or a description of it), and any optional context you choose to share (what isn't working, your LinkedIn URL, or your experience level). We do not collect this information passively — nothing is gathered until you submit the intake form yourself.

Why we collect it

The information and documents you submit are used only to prepare your Rithavo Application Diagnostic and to communicate with you about your order. We don't share, sell, or reuse your resume or personal information for any other purpose, and we don't use it to train external systems.

Third parties involved in processing your information

Our intake form is built and hosted on Tally, a third-party form provider — your submission, including your resume file, is processed and stored through Tally's platform before we retrieve it. We keep a private internal record of your submission (name, contact details, order details, and status) in Google Sheets, accessible only to Rithavo. To prepare your Application Diagnostic, your resume and target job description are processed using Anthropic's Claude API (an AI language model service) to assist with analysis; the resulting analysis and report are then reviewed by a person before anything is sent to you — nothing is delivered without human review. Anthropic's commercial API terms state that data submitted through the API is not used to train their models by default. Your generated diagnostic report and its underlying analysis are stored securely in Google Drive, accessible only to Rithavo, and are not shared publicly or with any other party. None of Tally, Google Sheets, Google Drive, or Anthropic's API is used by us for advertising or marketing purposes — they're used strictly as intake, analysis, and record-keeping infrastructure. Each of these providers' own handling of data is governed by their respective privacy policies.

How we handle payment

During this early stage, payment is handled manually by UPI transfer, with instructions provided directly to you after you submit the intake form. We do not collect card numbers, bank credentials, or UPI PINs through our website or forms — payment happens directly in your own banking app, outside of anything Rithavo or our form provider operates.

Consent

Before you can submit the intake form, you're asked to explicitly confirm that you consent to Rithavo processing the information and documents you've provided for the purpose of preparing your diagnostic. Submission is not possible without this confirmation.

How long we keep it

We retain your resume and intake information only as long as needed to deliver and support your order. You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing hello@rithavo.com, and we'll confirm once it's removed from both our records and, where applicable, from Tally.

Who has access

Your resume and information are accessed only as needed to prepare and quality-check your diagnostic. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your Rithavo account

If you create a Rithavo account, we store your email address, a securely hashed password if you set one, the education and experience you add to your Rithavo Profile, and records of your purchases, entitlements and reports. We use your email address to send sign-in and password links through our email provider (Zoho). Your account data is held in a managed database (Supabase) on our behalf, and this website is hosted on Vercel. Every request you make to this website passes through Vercel's infrastructure, which handles it in order to deliver the site and may keep its own standard request records under its own policies. Our email provider receives your email address and the text of the sign-in or password email we send you. When payments are switched on, they are processed by Cashfree: we pass your email address and phone number to Cashfree to create the order, we keep the order reference and its status, and we do not receive or store your card or UPI details. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by emailing hello@rithavo.com.

Cookies and analytics

Rithavo uses two first-party cookies. A sign-in cookie keeps you signed in after you log in; it is signed, marked Secure and HttpOnly, and lasts up to 14 days. An analytics cookie holds a random identifier for your browser and lasts up to one year. We use it to record a small set of events: a page view when you open one of our public pages, registration, sign-in, and clicks towards our two products. Each event stores the page path, the time, a coarse device type (mobile, tablet or desktop), the random identifier and, if you are signed in, your account. We do not store your IP address or your full browser details in these events, and we do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. Some third-party code does load in your browser. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads. The Career Intelligence and Application Diagnosis pages also load the payment provider Razorpay's checkout script as the page opens, so your browser contacts Razorpay when you open either page, whether or not you buy anything; what that script collects is governed by Razorpay's own privacy policy.

Ritanya, the Rithavo guide

Ritanya is a guide that answers questions about Rithavo and points you to the right page. She answers from fixed, reviewed product information. She is not an artificial-intelligence service: your messages are not sent to an AI provider or to any other party for processing, and she cannot see your account, your profile or your resume. When you send a message it is sent to rithavo.com and is used only, in memory, to choose a reply. We do not store the text of your message and we do not use it to identify you. Like every request to this website, your message passes through our hosting provider, Vercel, which may keep its own standard request records under its own policies. Ritanya sets no cookie. Your conversation is kept only in your browser's session storage for that tab; it is cleared when you choose New conversation, when you sign out, or when you close the tab. The guide also remembers in that tab whether it is switched on, so that it does not ask again on every page. For each message our server records only a technical entry (a request number, the outcome, a topic label and how long the reply took), never the message itself and never your address. To prevent abuse, your network address is held in the memory of the server that handles your message, only to count how many messages arrive in a short period. It is not saved to a database or written to our logs, and it is removed from memory within about ten minutes of your last message once that server handles another request, or sooner if the server restarts.

Using Rithavo as an installed app

You can add Rithavo to your device's home screen from rithavo.com. The installed app is the same website at the same address and uses the same sign-in cookie described above. To make pages load faster and to show a notice when you are offline, your browser keeps copies of Rithavo's public pages and files (for example the stylesheet, scripts and icons) in its storage. Pages that need you to be signed in are always loaded from the network and are never stored. We do not keep your profile, resume, results or any other account information in that storage, and the copies of pages you visited are removed when you sign out. You can remove everything by uninstalling the app or by clearing Rithavo's site data in your browser settings. This storage is not used to track you.

Your rights

  • You can ask what information we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • You can ask us to delete your information, subject to any order we're actively fulfilling for you.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to hello@rithavo.com.
