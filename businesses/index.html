Collaboration ideas

Ways we can work together.

Depending on the nature and scope of the work, a collaboration can run monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly. Start light, then go deeper as the partnership proves itself.

Monthly

Quick wins & low-friction experiments

Best for: Testing audience overlap, building immediate awareness and validating partnership chemistry without heavy legal or technical integration.

  • Co-hosted webinars or live panels: a 45-minute educational session with a complementary brand, with both promoting to their lists and sharing the leads.
  • Social media takeovers & giveaways: a joint giveaway, or a day of swapped Instagram and LinkedIn stories, to cross-pollinate audiences.
  • Newsletter swaps: a dedicated blurb, offer or top article in each other's newsletter.
  • Pop-up co-marketing: a local pairing, such as a book-and-espresso voucher valid for 30 days, to drive foot traffic.
Quarterly

Campaign-driven & lead-gen partnerships

Best for: Seasonal pushes, structured lead generation and bundles that need moderate coordination.

  • Bundled offerings: complementary products in one discounted package for a quarter.
  • Co-authored research or whitepapers: pooled, anonymised data or a co-funded survey, published as a flagship report both sales teams use.
  • Referral & affiliate programs: a tracked agreement paying a flat fee or commission for each qualified lead in a 90-day sprint.
  • Shared event sponsorships: split the cost of a booth or dinner, halving the spend and doubling the reach.
Half-yearly

Operational & product integrations

Best for: Deeper technical ties, expansion into adjacent markets and repeatable revenue channels.

  • API & product integrations: a native integration between two platforms, followed by a joint launch.
  • Co-branded product lines: a limited-edition product designed and made together, with six months to prototype, produce and launch.
  • Supply chain & bulk purchasing alliances: non-competing companies pooling volumes to win tiered supplier discounts.
  • White-labeling agreements: one company licenses its technology or service to another, who rebrands it as an add-on.
Yearly

Strategic alliances & ecosystem building

Best for: Long-term moats, high-capital commitments and transformational market expansion.

  • Joint ventures: pooled capital, intellectual property and talent in a distinct third entity, often to enter a new market.
  • Exclusive distribution or licensing: exclusive rights in a region or vertical for a 12-month renewable term.
  • Shared R&D and innovation labs: a co-funded long-term research initiative, with shared ownership of the results.
  • Cross-company talent exchange: an annual fellowship or rotation where specialists work inside the partner organisation.

The cadence shown is indicative. Every collaboration is scoped individually.
